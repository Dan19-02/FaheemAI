Since the ball is "dropped," we know its starting speed is zero, and it is moving downwards under the influence of gravity.

First, let's list what we have:
Initial velocity ($u$) = $0\text{ m/s}$ (because it is dropped)
Distance/Height ($s$) = $80\text{ m}$
Acceleration due to gravity ($g$) = $10\text{ m/s}^2$

**Step 1: Finding the time ($t$)**
We use the second equation of motion which relates displacement and time:
$$s = ut + \frac{1}{2}gt^2$$

Plugging in the values:
$$80 = (0)(t) + \frac{1}{2}(10)t^2$$
$$80 = 5t^2$$

To find $t^2$, we divide both sides by $5$:
$$t^2 = \frac{80}{5} = 16$$
$$t = \sqrt{16} = 4\text{ seconds}$$

**Step 2: Finding the final velocity ($v$)**
Now we use the first equation of motion to find the speed at the end:
$$v = u + gt$$

Substituting the values:
$$v = 0 + (10)(4)$$
$$v = 40\text{ m/s}$$

The ball takes 4 seconds to reach the ground and hits with a velocity of 40 m/s.
