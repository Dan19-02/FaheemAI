Jab light ek medium se doosre medium mein jaati hai, jaise air se water mein, toh uski speed badal jaati hai aur is wajah se light apna rasta mod leti hai, jise hum refraction kehte hain. 

Pencil ka jo hissa paani ke andar hai, usse reflect hone wali light rays pehle paani (denser medium) mein travel karti hain aur fir air (rarer medium) mein enter karti hain. Paani se air mein aate hi light ki speed badh jaati hai, jiski wajah se light rays normal se door bend ho jaati hain. 

Humara brain hamesha yeh assume karta hai ki light bilkul seedhi line mein chal kar humari aankhon tak aa rahi hai. Isliye, jab humari aankhein in bent rays ko peeche ki taraf trace karti hain, toh paani ke andar wala pencil ka part humein thoda upar utha hua (apparent depth) dikhta hai. Kyunki hawa wala part apni sahi jagah par dikhta hai aur paani wala part thoda upar dikhta hai, isliye pencil joint par bent nazar aati hai.

Isko samajhne ke liye ek simple shopping cart (trolley) ka example lete hain. Agar aap ek shopping cart ko smooth floor se kisi mote carpet par tedha (at an angle) push karein, toh jo wheel carpet par pehle pahuchega, uski speed pehle kam ho jayegi jabki doosra wheel abhi bhi smooth floor par tez chal raha hoga. Is speed ke difference ki wajah se cart ka rasta thoda mud (bend) jata hai. Yahan smooth floor air ki tarah hai, carpet water ki tarah hai, aur cart ka rasta light ray ki tarah hai.

Yeh concept humne Class 10 ke 'Light: Reflection and Refraction' chapter mein padha tha, aur yeh Class 12 ke 'Ray Optics' mein fir se aayega. 

Exam mein jab hum refractive index ke numericals solve karte hain, toh aksar speed aur angles ka ratio ulta likh dete hain. Hamesha yaad rakhna ki denser medium mein light ki speed hamesha kam hoti hai aur normal ke sath banne wala angle bhi chota hota hai.

Agar hum paani ki jagah ek zyada dense liquid jaise glycerin use karein, jismein light ki speed paani se bhi kam ho jaati hai, toh pencil humein kam bent dikhegi ya aur zyada bent dikhegi? Iske peeche ka apna reason zaroor batana.
