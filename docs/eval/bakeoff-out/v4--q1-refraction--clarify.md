Sunah raha hoon ki aaj class mein refraction samajh nahi aaya, koi baat nahi, yeh ek aisi cheez hai jahan almost har koi pehli baar thoda confuse hota hai, toh aap akele nahi ho.

Chaliye seedha uss phenomenon se shuru karte hain jo aapne khud dekha hai. Jab pencil paani mein hoti hai, toh pencil ke neeche ka hissa jo paani ke andar hai, woh thoda alag position se aapki aankh tak light bhejta hai, aur upar ka hissa jo hawa mein hai, woh seedha aata hai. Aapki aankh in dono hisson ko milake ek seedhi line banaati hai, isliye pencil ka woh point jahaan paani ki surface hai, wahan ek sudden shift dikhta hai, aur pencil "tedhi" lagti hai.

Ab asli sawaal: aisa kyun hota hai?

Yeh isliye hota hai kyunki light hawa aur paani mein alag speed se chalti hai. Hawa mein light lagbhag 3 lakh km/s chalti hai, paani mein thodi slow ho jaati hai. Jab light ek medium se doosre mein jaati hai (hawa se paani mein, ya paani se hawa mein), toh uski speed badal jaati hai, aur isi speed change ki wajah se light apna raasta thoda mod leti hai, yahi mod lena hi **refraction** hai.

Ek chhoti si baat jo Class 10 ke chapter "Light: Reflection and Refraction" mein bhi aati hai, aur yeh same idea Class 12 ke Ray Optics mein phir se milegi, toh ek baar clear ho gayi toh aage kaam aayegi.

Socho ek truck sadak se kheench mein aa raha hai, wheels ek taraf slow hoti hain, doosri taraf fast, toh truck apna raasta thoda tedha kar leta hai, light bhi kuch aisa hi karti hai jab hawa se paani mein jaati hai.

Ek cheez aur, paani se hawa mein aate waqt light **normal se door** modti hai, aur hawa se paani mein jaate waqt **normal ki taraf** modti hai. Normal ek imaginary line hoti hai jo surface ke bilkul 90 degree pe hoti hai.

Ab ek chhota sa check: agar pencil ko paani ki jagah ek aur taral cheez, jaise tel, mein daalein, toh kya pencil aur zyada tedi dikhegi, kam tedi dikhegi, ya utni hi dikhegi? Socho aur batao, reason bhi saath dena.
