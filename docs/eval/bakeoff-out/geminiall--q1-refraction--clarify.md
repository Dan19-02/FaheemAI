Pencil sach mein nahi mudti, balki paani ke andar se aane wali light rays jab bahar air mein aati hain, toh unki speed badhne ki wajah se wo bend ho jaati hain [Physics: Refraction of Light · Class 10].

Humari eyes ko lagta hai ki light hamesha bilkul seedhi line mein chalti hai. Jab paani ke andar wale hisse se light nikal kar air mein aati hai, toh wo normal se door (away from the normal) bend ho jaati hai [Physics: Refraction of Light · Class 10]. Humara brain in bend hui rays ko peeche ki taraf ek straight line mein trace karta hai, jisse pencil ka wo underwater hissa thoda upar utha hua (raised) dikhta hai [Physics: Refraction of Light · Class 10]. Isi illusion ki wajah se pencil hume bent ya mudi hui lagti hai [Physics: Refraction of Light · Class 10].

Isko samajhne ke liye ek simple daily life example lete hain. Maan lo aap ek toy car ko tiles wale floor se ek mote carpet par diagonal (tircha) chala rahe ho. Jaise hi car carpet par pahunchegi, uska ek wheel pehle carpet par aayega aur slow ho jayega, jabki doosra wheel abhi bhi tiles par tez chal raha hoga. Is speed difference ki wajah se car thodi ghum (bend ho) jaati hai. Light ke sath bhi bilkul yahi hota hai jab wo water se air mein aati hai.

Yeh topic Class 10 ke 'Light: Reflection and Refraction' chapter mein padhaya jata hai aur Class 12 ke 'Ray Optics and Optical Instruments' mein yeh fir se lautega [Physics: Refraction in the CBSE/NCERT syllabus · CBSE · Class 10]. Ek common mistake jo kaafi students karte hain wo yeh sochna hai ki light denser medium (jaise water) mein enter karte hi fast ho jaati hai. Hamesha yaad rakhna, denser medium mein light ki speed kam hoti hai aur rarer medium (jaise air) mein speed badh jaati hai, isliye jab light water se air mein aati hai toh wo normal se door bend hoti hai, paas nahi [Physics: Refraction of Light · Class 10].
