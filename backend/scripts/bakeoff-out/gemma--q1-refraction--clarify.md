Light travels at different speeds depending on the medium it is moving through. When light rays coming from the pencil enter the water from air, they slow down because water is denser than air, and this change in speed causes the rays to bend.

Imagine a car moving from a smooth road onto soft grass at an angle; the wheels hitting the grass slow down first, causing the car to turn slightly. way brain assumes light always travels in a perfectly straight line, so when the rays bend in the water, our eyes perceive the pencil as being in a different position than where it actually is.

If light moves from water into air, will it bend towards the normal or away from the normal?
