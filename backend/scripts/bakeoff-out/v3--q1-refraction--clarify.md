Socho, light tumhari aankh tak paunchne ke liye ek seedhi raasta follow karti hai. Ab jab wo raasta paani aur hawa ke beech ka border cross karta hai, toh uski speed badal jaati hai, paani mein slow ho jaati hai. Speed badalne se uska raasta bhi thoda mod leta hai, jaise ek gaadi jab dheeli mitti pe aati hai toh thodi tedi ho jaati hai.

Ab pencil ka wo hissa jo paani mein hai, usse aane wali light bhi isi mod ke saath travel karti hai. Isliye tumhari aankh ko lagta hai ki pencil ka neeche ka hissa thoda alag jagah se aa raha hai, aur upar ka hissa seedha. Dono hisson ko jodne par pencil ko todti hui, yaani tedhi dikhti hai.

Yahi reason hai ki coins paani ke andar apni asli position se thoda upar dikhte hain (apparent depth kam ho jaati hai), aur swimming pool ki depth asli se kam dikhti hai.

Ab tum khud ek chhota sa check karo: agar pencil ko paani ki jagah kisi aur denser cheez jaise honey ya oil mein daalo, toh kya zyada bend dikhegi ya kam? Sochke batao, kyunki yahi Snell's law ka asli test hai.
