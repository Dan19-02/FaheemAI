Ball drop karte hain, toh u = 0, h = 80 m, g = 10 m/s².

**Time pakadne ke liye:**

$$h = \tfrac{1}{2} g t^2$$

$$80 = \tfrac{1}{2} \times 10 \times t^2$$

$$t^2 = 16 \implies t = 4 \text{ s}$$

**Velocity just before hitting the ground:**

$$v = g \cdot t = 10 \times 4 = 40 \text{ m/s}$$

**Cross-check:** $v^2 = 2gh = 2 \times 10 \times 80 = 1600$, so $v = 40$ m/s. Same answer, units sahi hain.

**Final answer:** t = 4 s, v = 40 m/s (downward)

Yeh wahi free-fall setup hai jo Motion in a Straight Line mein padha tha, bas numbers alag hain. Ek common slip hai yahan: "dropped" word dekhke kabhi kabhi u = 0 bhool jaate hain, aur phir galat formula lagate hain. Dropped ka matlab hi starting velocity zero hai.
