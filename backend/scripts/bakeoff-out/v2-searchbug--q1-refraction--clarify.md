Pencil paani mein bent isliye dikhti hai kyunki paani se nikal kar humari aankhon tak aane wali light rays seedhi nahi chalti, balki paani aur hawa ke border par thoda sa mud (bend ho) jaati hain.

Hawa ke mukable paani optically denser hota hai, jisme light ki speed kam ho jaati hai. Jab light rays paani ke andar dube hue pencil ke hisse se nikal kar hawa mein aati hain, toh unki speed achanak badh jaati hai. Is speed change ki wajah se light rays seedhi jaane ke bajaye normal (surface ke perpendicular khichi gayi ek imaginary line) se door bend ho jaati hain.

Humara dimaag hamesha yeh assume karta hai ki light ekdum seedhi line se aa rahi hai. Isliye jab hum in bended rays ko piche ki taraf seedha trace karte hain, toh pencil ka duba hua hissa apni asli position se thoda upar utha hua dikhta hai, jisse pencil surface par se bent nazar aati hai.

Isko samajhne ke liye ek shopping cart ka example lete hain. Agar tum ek cart ko smooth floor se mitti (sand) par ek angle par dhakelte ho, toh jo wheel mitti ko pehle touch karega uski speed pehle kam ho jayegi, jabki baaki wheels abhi bhi smooth floor par tez chal rahe honge. Is speed ke difference ki wajah se cart ka rasta bend ho jata hai. Light ke sath bhi bilkul aisa hi hota hai jab wo ek medium se doosre medium mein jaati hai.

**Exam Edge:** Exams mein ek bohot common mistake jo students karte hain, wo hai physical density (mass per unit volume) aur optical density (light ko bend karne ki ability) ko same samajh lena. Kerosene ki physical density paani se kam hoti hai (isliye wo paani ke upar tairta hai), lekin uski optical density paani se zyada hoti hai, yaani usme light paani se bhi zyada bend hoti hai.

Agar hum paani ki jagah koi aisa liquid use karein jo paani se bhi zyada optically dense ho (jaise glycerin), toh pencil humein kam bent dikhegi ya aur bhi zyada bent dikhegi? Light ki speed ke change ko soch kar guess karo.
