# Faheem

## Product description

Faheem is an Arabic-first AI tutor built for Bahrain's secondary students (grades 7 to 12). It answers a student's question in clear, natural Arabic, in English, or in both, grounded in the exact Bahrain Ministry of Education curriculum unit they are studying, and it shows its source.

Every explanation is taught, not just told. Faheem starts from intuition, works through the steps, uses a familiar local example, and checks its own work before the student ever sees it. When an answer does not land, one tap re-explains it a completely different way, until it does.

Accuracy is the product. Faheem stays inside the student's syllabus and flags anything outside it, so what a student learns at night matches what the exam asks in the morning.

**One line:** An Arabic-first AI tutor that teaches Bahrain's national curriculum, grounded and sourced, so students learn exactly what their exam will ask.
