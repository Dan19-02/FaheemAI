# Faheem — Head-to-Head Evaluation vs. Frontier Models

**Does Faheem actually beat handing a Bahraini student a raw frontier model?**
We tested it — blind, on real Bahrain MoE curriculum questions, graded by an expert
panel. Faheem's own answers are in the ring against **real Google Gemini** and raw Claude.

> ### ⚠️ Read this first — what this test is, and isn't
>
> A **controlled benchmark to validate Faheem's evaluation framework and compare it to
> leading models** — not a live classroom trial or a peer-reviewed study.
> - **Students & graders are AI personas.** The 10 students and 5 teacher-graders are
>   AI-generated, each built on a **real Bahrain MoE curriculum unit** (grades 9–12) —
>   not real children or licensed teachers.
> - **The competitor answers are REAL.** Gemini answers were generated by Google's live
>   API — **`gemini-3.1-pro-preview`** and **`gemini-2.5-pro`**. (`gemini-3-pro` is
>   retired and no `gemini-3.5-pro` exists on the key; DeepSeek needs its own key and
>   was not reachable — so the two best available real Gemini models were used.)
> - **"Faheem AI" = the product's approach**, i.e. Claude Opus 4.8 running Faheem's full
>   spec (Arabic-first + MoE-grounded + sourced + verify + re-explain). The raw models
>   got a fair generic-assistant prompt. The deployed backend adds real corpus RAG on
>   top, so production grounding should be equal or stronger.
> - **Blind & fair.** Each grader read a packet of four anonymized answers (A–D), order
>   rotated per student, and scored on a fixed 1–10 rubric.
> - **Validate before external claims.** These numbers show relative quality and a
>   working method; re-run with **real Bahraini students and teachers** before marketing.

---

## Method

**10 students** (5 Arabic · 3 English · 2 bilingual, grades 9–12, real MoE units) ×
**4 systems** = 40 answers, each scored by **5 teachers** (incl. a native Arabic
teacher) = **200 blind gradings**. Rubric, 1–10: **Accuracy · Curriculum alignment ·
Language quality · Pedagogy · Trust & sourcing.**

Systems: **Faheem AI** (grounded, Arabic-first) · **Gemini 3.1 Pro** (raw) ·
**Gemini 2.5 Pro** (raw) · **Claude Opus 4.8** (raw). Faheem and raw Opus share the
same base engine, so their gap isolates the **Faheem product layer**.

## Results — the head-to-head

| System | Overall | Accuracy | Curriculum | Language | Pedagogy | Trust/Src | Best-answer wins |
|---|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|
| 🥇 **Faheem AI** (grounded) | **8.96** | 9.2 | **9.3** | 8.8 | **9.4** | **8.2** | **43 / 50** |
| 🥈 Claude Opus 4.8 (raw) | 8.32 | 9.3 | 8.1 | 8.6 | 8.5 | 7.0 | 6 |
| 🥉 Gemini 2.5 Pro (raw) | 8.00 | 9.2 | 7.8 | 8.3 | 8.0 | 6.7 | 1 |
| Gemini 3.1 Pro (raw) | 7.61 | 9.0 | 7.4 | 8.3 | 7.0 | 6.4 | 0 |

**Faheem leads the field: +0.96 over Gemini 2.5 Pro, +1.35 over Gemini 3.1 Pro**, and
won the best-answer vote on **43 of 50** gradings. Across the 10 students, Faheem won
**8**; raw Opus won **2** (both English STEM); **Gemini never won a single student.**

### The honest shape of the win

Faheem does **not** win on raw brainpower — on pure **accuracy it's a tie** (Faheem 9.2,
raw Opus 9.3, Gemini 2.5 Pro 9.2). It wins on exactly what the product is built for:

| Dimension | Faheem | best raw competitor | Faheem's edge |
|---|:--:|:--:|:--:|
| Curriculum alignment | **9.3** | 8.1 (Opus) | **+1.2** |
| Pedagogy | **9.4** | 8.5 (Opus) | **+0.9** |
| Trust & sourcing | **8.2** | 7.0 (Opus) | **+1.2** |
| Arabic — native-teacher score | **9.0** | 8.2 (Opus) | **+0.8** |
| Accuracy | 9.2 | 9.3 (Opus) | −0.1 (tie) |

The takeaway for the pitch: **frontier models are already smart; Faheem makes that
intelligence land for a Bahraini student** — grounded in their unit, sourced, taught,
and in genuinely native Arabic.

### Where the raw models did better (stated plainly)

- **Raw Opus beat Faheem on two English STEM questions** — projectile motion (Grade 11)
  and DNA replication (Grade 12) — where the answer is pure content and Faheem's
  Arabic-first grounding adds least. Honest, and expected.
- **Every system computed the physics correctly** (e.g. `a = 5 m/s²`, projectile range
  `35.3 m`) — raw accuracy was never the differentiator.
- **Surprise:** the newer **Gemini 3.1 Pro scored *below* Gemini 2.5 Pro** here (7.61 vs
  8.00) — terser, less pedagogically warm, and it **hallucinated an unsupported "five
  days" exam timeline** on one question, which graders penalized on trust.

## Supporting result — isolating the Faheem layer

A separate blind run pitted **Faheem (Opus 4.8 + product layer)** against the **same raw
Opus 4.8** and the raw Claude field (Sonnet 5, Haiku 4.5), 250 gradings:

| System | Overall | Faheem won |
|---|:--:|:--:|
| **Faheem AI** | **9.1** | **39 / 50** |
| Claude Opus 4.8 (raw) | 8.4 | — |
| Claude Sonnet 5 (raw) | 8.1 | — |
| Claude Haiku 4.5 (raw) | 6.7 | — |

Same engine, **+0.7 from the Faheem layer alone** — concentrated in trust/sourcing
(+1.4), pedagogy (+1.1), and curriculum (+1.0). Proof the lift is the *product*, not
just the model.

## Limitations & next steps

1. **Simulated participants** — personas + AI graders, not real students/teachers. Next:
   a pilot with real Grade-10 Physics students and 3–5 real Bahraini teachers.
2. **Faheem = model + spec**, not the full deployed RAG+verify backend — evaluate the
   live system end-to-end next.
3. **One question per student**; a fuller study needs several per unit plus deliberate
   out-of-syllabus probes.
4. **Not reached:** DeepSeek v4 (needs its own key); `gemini-3-pro`/`3.5-pro`
   unavailable. Re-run with those when access exists.

**Reproducibility:** blind, order-rotated packets; raw answers, per-dimension scores,
and the label→system mapping are in [`docs/eval/`](docs/eval/) (`head-to-head-answers.json`,
`head-to-head-scores.json`).
